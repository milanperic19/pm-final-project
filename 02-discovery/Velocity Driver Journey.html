The driver's day, one tap at a time

Velocity gives every role a home screen built around the jobs they do most. For drivers, starting a route and marking a stop delivered takes one tap, so the app is faster than texting dispatch or carrying a paper backup. Dispatchers benefit too: when drivers complete stops in the app, the board stays accurate.

Persona

Experienced delivery driver working against the clock on dozens of stops a day, often in poor weather or weak signal.

Goal

Get through each stop as fast as possible, with the app keeping pace instead of slowing them down.

Today's workaround

Skips the three-screen flow and texts dispatch instead; keeps a paper manifest as backup.

Why it beats texting dispatch

  • One tap beats a text

    Faster for the driver, so there's no reason to work around the app.

  • A structured record

    Each delivery is logged with a timestamp, location and photo, not a loose message.

  • No double handling

    Dispatchers stop relaying texts, and reporting gets complete data.

Scope note: stages 1 and 3 come from the role-based home screen. Stage 2 (push alerts) and reliable sync in stage 4 also depend on fixes to sync and reliability (BUG-2044, BUG-2072).
