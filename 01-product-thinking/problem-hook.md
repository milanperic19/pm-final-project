# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** RouteLogic
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** We could see increased customer churn (especially with large enterprise accounts), which would result in a decreased market share as users turn to leaner competitors that manage to position themselves as leaders in the market. The extensiveness of the feature list becomes less important as the buyers start comparing providers not based on the list of features but on how fast the provider enables daily work to get done.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** The customers are then likely using a variety of other tools (or one tool, from a competitor?) to get their key work done. Eventually, they will turn to tools that better serve their key needs, eroding RouteLogic platform's value.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** Our most powerful product has become our slowest one, as frontline users shift to other tools to avoid the complexity of our platform, and we are at risk of losing our enterprise accounts to leaner competitors.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** RouteLogic Velocity gives every role a home screen built around the work they do most. Drivers start routes and mark stops delivered in one tap, making the app faster than texting dispatch, and dispatchers get a board they can trust. Enterprise reporting and compliance stay in the product for the roles that need them, out of the way for those that don’t. We need to launch now: our largest customer is already trialling a leaner competitor.
## Cold-read your own hook
- **Is the business risk high enough to justify a new initiative, a high-stakes threat, or a minor inconvenience?:** Considering increased churn risk due to decreasing platform usage and product-market (mis)fit, velocity initiatives founded on and informed by user research are essential if the company wants to maintain its competitive positioning.
- **Is the moment of misery a systemic problem or just an edge case?:** If the platform usage is consistently low or decreasing and the enterprise account described in this task is a good representation of our typical user, then it is a systemic problem. If the enterprise user's behaviour does not match the behavior of all our other users, then it could be an edge case. This should be validated through user interviews and usage analysis.
- **Does the value proposition actually remove the obstacle you identified, or is it a generic feature?:** The proposition aims to directly link the user's needs with the platform's features, to shape how these are delivered to and accessed by the customer. It aims to solve the problem by a) analysing usage and custome patterns to identify key features and b) using this to reorganise spaces on the platform based on key jobs to be done, so that the platform is calibrated to the needs of those who use it the most.
